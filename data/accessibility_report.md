## Classification doubts

- Unmatched HF video files were not auto-added in this run. Only existing catalog and candidate rows can be promoted. Pass --add-unmatched to include them.
- moss/ and *-moss.safetensors were treated as image (KroMoss / Krea) unless an exact catalog video-row match already used the file.
- Official Hugging Face packs (lightx2v, Lightricks IC-LoRAs, etc.) stay in candidates.jsonl unless the same weights were also uploaded to Octavian123/civit, Octavian123/civit2, or Octavian123/private, or are on the RAID. They are scored high for ingest because they fill utility gaps, but they are not treated as already accessible.
- Version forks (e.g. `…-v2-h3` on RAID vs `…-h3` on Graydient) are not merged. Each slug is its own row. When Graydient registered a concept under a distinct slug (for example `minimax-h3-t2v-i2v-doublefuck-h3-v2`), that name is stored in `graydient_slug` on the catalog row.
- Exact slug/file matches are kept even when the RAID family and the filename suffix disagree (H3 ports whose slugs still end in `-wan`).
