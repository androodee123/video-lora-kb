# Fuzz Puppy video LoRA directory

Snapshot of [fuzzpuppy.com/lora-directory](https://www.fuzzpuppy.com/lora-directory) taken 13 Nov 2026. 61 tracked LoRAs, mostly official or utility adapters for MiniMax H3, LTX-2 / 2.3 / 2.5, and one Wan 2.2 entry. They are included as a **lead for commonly used setups**, not as installed RAID inventory.

Almost none of the NSFW act LoRAs on the RAID appear here. A few H3 names overlap in *role* (360 orbit, turbo, realism) but the installed filenames differ; do not assume the RAID file is the Fuzz Puppy weight.
